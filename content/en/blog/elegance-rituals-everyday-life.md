## Elegance lies in the details

Elegance is not a matter of grand occasions. It settles in the repetition of small gestures that transform the everyday without changing its substance.

## The morning ritual

A real cup of coffee or tea taken seated, in a chosen porcelain, makes all the difference compared to a mug downed standing up. Breakfast is a short but decisive moment: it sets the tone for the day. A few minutes are enough, but they must be taken for oneself.

## The dressing ritual

Choosing your clothes the night before frees the morning mind and avoids default choices. A perfume applied even to stay home, polished shoes, a coordinated outfit even on an informal day: these gestures signal to the body that the day has truly begun.

## The table ritual

Eating at a real table, with chosen cutlery, a fabric napkin rather than paper, a stemmed glass even for water. These details take the same time as the standard version but transform the experience of the meal.

## The evening ritual

A book rather than a screen before sleeping, a tidy bedroom, dimmed lighting. The evening ritual prepares for sleep but it also symbolizes the closing of a day: setting an intention on the end of the day, as one set one on its beginning.

## The reading ritual

Fifteen minutes of reading each day, always at the same moment, in the same armchair if possible. Regularity creates a mental space of reflection that then extends throughout the day.

## Why rituals matter

An elegance ritual is not a staging of oneself. It is a frame that structures time and reminds, through repetition, that one has chosen one's way of living. It is less a question of objective beauty than a matter of consistency: choosing to do things with care, because they deserve that care.

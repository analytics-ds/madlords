## Why the Shamballa Jewels bracelet appeals to collectors

The **Shamballa Jewels bracelet** holds a distinct place in contemporary jewelry. Founded in Copenhagen in 2005 by brothers Mads and Mikkel Kornerup, the house built its reputation on one specific object: the macramé braided bracelet, whose evenly sized beads are hand-assembled using a knotting technique inherited from traditional craft. This choice turned a spiritual accessory into a piece of fine jewelry.

The brand's story is tied to a founding commission, a bracelet designed for the rapper Jay-Z, which popularized the knotted gold-bead model. The house then created its first diamond pavé bead, which became an instantly recognizable signature. Every bracelet is now handcrafted in Denmark, which explains both its price and its desirability.

This artisanal standard won over a prestigious clientele. Karl Lagerfeld, Crown Princess Mary of Denmark and Giorgio Armani have been among the house's collectors, which durably established the Shamballa Jewels bracelet within the luxury world. This heritage dimension echoes the logic of a considered purchase, as with a [heritage jewel](/en/blog/how-to-choose-an-heirloom-piece/) meant to be passed down.

### The criteria for choosing a Shamballa Jewels bracelet

Five criteria structure the choice of a **Shamballa Jewels bracelet** and, by extension, the site to buy it from:

- **Material**: beads come in 18-carat gold, silver, ceramic and gemstones, with white or black diamond pavé on the most elaborate models. Material directly drives the price.
- **Model**: the house offers several families, from the braided Braided bracelet to the Lock model with engraved square beads, as well as Non-Braided versions. Each family corresponds to a different intensity of style.
- **Bead size**: common diameters range from 6 to 10 millimeters. A 10-millimeter bead paved with diamonds represents a far higher budget than a 6-millimeter bead in a single stone.
- **Authenticity**: every genuine piece is handcrafted in Copenhagen and numbered. The authenticity guarantee is the decisive criterion when choosing a purchase site.
- **Budget**: count from 2,600 euros for an entry-level model up to over 37,000 euros for a piece fully paved with diamonds.

The same traceability requirements apply as when buying a [diamond bracelet](/en/blog/diamond-bracelet-under-800-euros/), where stone certification and the house signature build the piece's value over time.

### International authorized retailers

Several authorized jewelers officially distribute Shamballa Jewels, notably TWISTonline, The Jewellery Room and The Loupe. These retailers provide an authenticity guarantee and a selection of gold, silver and diamond bracelets, but their Shamballa Jewels catalog remains partial and often oriented toward the North American market.

### Generalist marketplaces, to avoid for genuine Shamballa Jewels

Marketplaces like Amazon are full of bracelets sold under the term shamballa for a few dozen euros. The vast majority are style imitations, with rhinestone or resin beads, with no connection to the Shamballa Jewels house and its Danish artisanal manufacturing.

These products offer no authenticity guarantee and should not be confused with a genuine **Shamballa Jewels bracelet**. For an authentic purchase, it is better to turn to one of the three other channels in the comparison.

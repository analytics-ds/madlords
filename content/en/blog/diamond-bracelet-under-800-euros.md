## Practical Tips for Buying a Diamond Bracelet at 800 Euros

Systematically check metal purity, indicated by a hallmark (925 for sterling silver, 375 for 9-karat gold, 585 for 14-karat gold, 750 for 18-karat gold). Request the **diamond certificate** above 0.20 carat (GIA, IGI or HRD). **Check the origin of the stone**: natural or laboratory, the information must be explicit in the product description. Check the clasp mechanism, the weak point of fine bracelets.

Jewelry investment also follows trends: see our dossier on [2026 trends in fine jewelry](/en/blog/2026-trends-fine-jewelry/) to understand which styles are gaining value.

### Mistakes to Avoid

1. Compare only the total diamond weight without looking at origin (natural or laboratory)
2. Neglect metal purity, which determines the durability and value of the setting
3. Buy without verifying after-sales service options, especially for interchangeable cords

<details>
<summary>Is a lab-grown diamond worth a natural diamond?</summary>

Optically and chemically, a lab-grown diamond is identical to a natural diamond: same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), same refractive index, same properties. The difference lies in economic and heritage value. A synthetic diamond loses 60 to 80 percent at resale according to the GIA. It is perfectly suitable for personal use, less so for investment or transmission.

</details>

<details>
<summary>Is a 925 silver bracelet as durable as gold for daily wear?</summary>

Sterling 925 silver is composed of 92.5 percent pure silver and 7.5 percent other metals to strengthen it. Its hardness is lower than gold, but it withstands careful daily use well. Main drawback: it oxidizes on contact with air and requires regular maintenance (soft cloth, avoid prolonged contact with water and perfumes). Advantage for the Madlords Love Lab line: the nylon cord protects part of the metal from skin contact, limiting oxidation.

</details>

The most significant gap between the four brands concerns **gold purity**. Madlords works almost exclusively in 18-karat gold, which contains 75 percent pure gold. ## Practical Tips for Buying a Diamond Bracelet at 800 Euros

Systematically check gold purity, indicated by a hallmark (375 for 9-karat, 585 for 14-karat, 750 for 18-karat). Request the **diamond certificate** above 0.20 carat (GIA, IGI or HRD). Check the clasp mechanism, the weak point of fine bracelets. For pre-owned, request **full traceability**: provenance, date of acquisition by the seller, declared condition.

Jewelry investment also follows trends: see our dossier on [2026 trends in fine jewelry](/en/blog/2026-trends-fine-jewelry/) to understand which styles are gaining value.

### Mistakes to Avoid

1. Compare only the total diamond weight without looking at quality (color, clarity, cut)
2. Neglect gold purity, which determines the durability and value of the setting
3. Buy pre-owned without formal written authentication, which protects in case of resale

<summary>Is a lab-grown diamond worth a natural diamond?</summary>

Optically and chemically, a lab-grown diamond is identical to a natural diamond: same hardness (10 on the Mohs scale), same refractive index, same properties. The difference lies in economic and heritage value. A synthetic diamond loses 60 to 80 percent at resale according to the GIA. It is perfectly suitable for personal use, less so for investment or transmission.

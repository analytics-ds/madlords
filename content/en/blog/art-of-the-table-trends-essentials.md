## A set table tells a story

The art of the table is not a question of complication. A beautiful table comes down to a few carefully chosen pieces, in a harmony that reflects the home itself.

## The timeless essentials

### White tableware

White porcelain remains the basis of any elegant table. Limoges, Meissen, Bernardaud or Royal Copenhagen: these houses offer models that never go out of fashion. A flat plate, a soup plate, a bread plate per guest: that is the minimum for a refined table.

### Silver or premium stainless steel cutlery

A quality cutlery set spans generations. Solid silver requires maintenance but offers an incomparable patina. High-end stainless steel offers a more practical alternative without sacrificing elegance.

### The stemmed glass

A wine glass, a water glass, possibly a flute: three glasses are enough for most occasions. Choose classic hand-blown crystal models rather than a multiplication of specific shapes.

### Table linen

A placemat or a washed linen tablecloth completely changes the perception of a table. Egyptian cotton and European linen remain the references. Neutral colors (ecru, off-white, pearl gray) marry with all tableware.

## The 2026 trends

Several movements run through table setting this year:

- **The return of color**: colored tableware (sage green, terracotta, kiln blue) returns in capsule collections from major houses.
- **Unique artisanal pieces**: signed ceramics, hand-blown glasses, forged cutlery. The artisan's gesture regains value.
- **Mixing eras**: making an antique plate dialogue with contemporary cutlery becomes a signature.
- **The return of the full service**: after years of minimalism, multi-piece services are claimed again.

## Composing your own table

The classic mistake is to want everything to match. A beautiful table plays on contrasts: fine porcelain and thick raw linen napkin, crystal glass and artisanal carafe. It is this tension that makes a table memorable, rather than an entirely coordinated service.
